Right hand and wrist anatomy pack for mod_aianatomy

Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP).
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en

Citation: Mitsuhashi N, Fujieda K, Tamura T, Kawamoto S, Takagi T, Okubo K.
BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts.
Nucleic Acids Res. 2009;37(Database issue):D782-5. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Source files: BodyParts3D version 3.0 (20110915), obtained as binary STL from
https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D (converted from the original OBJ files).

Modifications (model.glb): 29 structures combined into one GLB, one node per structure;
vertices welded; smooth normals computed; axes converted (X patient-left, Y superior,
Z anterior, millimetres); recentred on the hand. Geometry is otherwise unchanged.
Node names are neutral (s0..s28); pack.json maps each node to its stable id and FMA id.

The converted model.glb is distributed under the same licence (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP).
